Research suggests bounded chunks, geometric blades, spatial color variation and
controlled distance handling as applicable techniques. This is not performance
validation of those repositories or permission to copy unspecified assets. Our
implementation uses ordinary installed Bevy0.17.3 Mesh/StandardMaterial and
VisibilityRange APIs. All geometry/color functions are authored here; no new
asset files, external dependency or vendored source. Existing project MIT terms
apply to original code. No blanket legal clearance is claimed.

`reference-images.json` records filenames, dimensions and SHA256 of all five
inspected attachments; their image files are not copied into the repository.
`docs/third_party/ASSETS.csv` records the original landscape generator and refreshes
only the three changed existing Metropolis source entries. Ground flecks are
generated by the new original source, not downloaded textures.
