# Integration recommendations

Decision: promote existing original model families to stable inventory identities
with independently authored bounded abilities, while preserving the already
accepted direct-hit and physical response contract. All five share the explicit
club contact keys until specialist authored animation review. Avoid unvalidated
flexible-body weapon physics, new knockoff energy, engine migration or copied
reference material. Keep compatible pinned Bevy0.17.3/Rapier0.32.0 and lockfile.

Alternatives: retain cosmetic-only models, or invent full flexible attacks and
hit multipliers in one pass. Typed family data provides working pickups, drops,
rival loadouts, readability and distinct reach/cost/recovery without changing the
accepted physical rig. It does not finish the full arsenal/art/animation gate.

Compatibility: Club identity remains unchanged, runtime weapons are not profile
fields, schema1 Club catalogs remain loadable with safe defaults for missing
family entries, schema2 explicitly authors every family. No new crate/assets or
license change. Existing original GLB catalog hashes are retained.

Rollback: restore the five-family content/core/runtime files and native fixture
hook together; original Club behavior/assets remain available throughout.

Checklist: keep V2-07-001/002/003/004, ARCH-004, RIGHTS-011, P05-004/005/007/008
open. Update their partial notes with typed five-family combat/loadouts/contact
and native fixture coverage only after actual validation. Recommend no new task
ID for this incremental work: every change maps to existing V2-07-001/002/003,
ARCH-004 and P05-007. New requirements have not been invented or deleted.

Known limits: shared side-swing silhouette, rigid chain/whip geometry, no holster
or steal/backhand system, manual viewer LOD only, subjective combat balance and
full three-minute weapon/unarmed loop, controller/hardware and GPU/long-session
performance remain open. Model GLB readiness uses the existing resident baton
fallback; fallback is not final family art. No claim of historical native weapon
parity or legal clearance.
