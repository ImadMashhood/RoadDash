# Role-free rider lines and incident combat

Branch: `codex/rider-free-roam` from `origin/main` `3732f179` (Bevy 0.17.3 control). ADR159; RFU-AI-09..11. This policy revision is separate from the Bevy 0.20 migration.

## Cause and implementation

Rival planning entered `ai_outbound_bounds` and `plan_with_oncoming`, so a clear opposite road still required a second permission forecast. Close combat also retained seven fixed Racer and seven Killer roles. The new rider path uses full physical road bounds plus 2.4 m Metropolis sidewalk and a narrow outer margin. The curved static shape sweep checks candidate line changes against solid props/buildings, while the predictive traffic envelope still checks cars and other upright riders. Civilian lane policy is unchanged. The full corridor is sampled over 120 m; the old 720 m oncoming width minimum is not used for riders.

All fourteen rivals use their authored race pace. Nearby eligible riders can trigger opportunistic attacks; target intent never matches a slow target's speed. A confirmed hit rolls a reproducible 50/50 bit from rider ID, attacker ID and incident serial. Fight permits nearby retaliation; flee suppresses attacks for eight seconds, picks a line away from a nearby attacker and requests a small bounded speed increase subject to curve, traffic and collision caps. Police continue to target only the wanted player. Save schema, controls, physics rig, fixed schedule, recovery and civilian traffic policy were not deliberately changed.
