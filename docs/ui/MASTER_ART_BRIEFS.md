For fullscreen backgrounds,3840×2160 is a proposed RoadDash master, not the original4:3 size. Preserve key subjects inside the central4:3 region until layout approval; keep the5% outer safe area free of text. Other pieces keep their source aspect as a suggested4× master. Use transparent PNG for cutouts, layers/masks for editable sources, and live/vector type and meters.
