# Offline slice contract v1

Locked design target, 2026-10-30 (Part 00). This defines future acceptance, not
completion of Parts 02-09.

- One authored approximately three-minute town -> farm -> cliff/coastal route.
- One working bike tuning ID and two cosmetic variants, original art only.
- Player plus at least three opponents, live traffic and fair shared controls.
- Punches and kicks, readable damage/knockoff, independent physical rider/bike.
- Ragdoll -> get up -> independent manual walking; neutral assisted return,
  safe remount, no forced return while the player deliberately walks away.
- Valid ordered forward checkpoint sequence; bike or rider may finish once.
- Clock, placement, readable HUD, results/payouts and immediate retry.
- Save safety, original/cleared audio, supported keyboard/controller UX.
- Fedora and Windows builds; stable 60 Hz simulation independent of rendering.

Evidence needed to accept: automated rule/regression tests, complete recordings
including foot finish, ten external play reports, measured target-hardware frame
times, provenance inventory and an explicit review of unmet P0 requirements.

No original maps, 15-bike roster, complete career, online play, photorealism,
weather, customization or modding is required to *build* this slice. Police and
full garage remain P1 while the blueprint P09 combined gate still calls for
police: that larger gate stays open until its explicit requirement is satisfied.
