# Source and transformation record

2026-11-06 — RoadDash contributors, original derivative geometry under repository
MIT. No new third-party code/media acquired. Source: the existing original authored
100 facade fronts and 28 whole structure models in ../front_modules and ../models.
Their previous research/AI concept provenance remains documented in the parent
catalog; this export does not establish clearance of likeness or a production art
grant beyond that record.

Transformations: clip original front triangles into source-generator floor bands;
interpolate normals/UVs at cuts; rebase scene origins; exact tuple vertex dedup;
extract identical embedded own-material PNG bytes to shared hashed files. Structure
fronts use the shallow front region; they are not claimed to be full structure
replacements. Original source GLBs and materials are not modified.

MANIFEST.json provides source/export SHA256 per LOD, bounds/triangle counts per
scene and nominal band dimensions. SHA256SUMS covers each derived GLB/PNG. Rebuild
with tools/build_architecture_kit.py and independently verify with
 tools/verify_architecture_kit.py from the repository root.
